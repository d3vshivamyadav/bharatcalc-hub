Complete Financial Reference Guide (Tax Year 2026–27)

How to Calculate Income Tax, GST 2.0, SIP Returns, HRA, FD & NPS in India

Whether you are a salaried professional comparing Old vs New Tax Regimes for Form 12BB declarations, a business owner billing under GST 2.0 slabs, or an investor building long-term mutual fund and retirement wealth, BharatCalc Hub computes accurate figures locally in your browser with instant A4 PDF and Word (DOCX) exports.

1. Old vs New Tax Regime (TY 2026–27 / AY 2027–28)

Under the revised New Tax Regime (Sec 202 / 115BAC), salaried employees claim a ₹75,000 Standard Deduction and pay zero tax on net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 via the enhanced ₹60,000 tax rebate—making gross salary up to ₹12.75 Lakh completely tax-free. Employer NPS contribution under Section 80CCD(2) up to 14% of Basic + DA is deductible in both regimes.

2. GST 2.0 Invoice Calculator (CGST, SGST & IGST)

Seamlessly toggle between GST Exclusive (+ Add GST to Net Base) and GST Inclusive (Reverse Extract GST from MRP) across GST 2.0 brackets (0% Exempt, 3% Gold/Bullion, 5% Merit Essentials, 18% Standard Services/Electronics, and 40% Demerit/Luxury) plus historical 12% and 28% rates. Intra-state supplies split tax 50:50 into CGST and SGST.

3. Step-Up SIP & Lumpsum Compounding Planner

Mutual fund Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) compound monthly using M = P × [((1 + i)^n - 1) / i] × (1 + i). Adding a 10% annual Step-Up SIP aligned with annual salary increments significantly accelerates wealth creation. Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG u/s 112A) on equity funds enjoy a ₹1,25,000 annual exemption and are taxed at 12.5% thereafter.

4. HRA Rule 2A, FD Sec 194A TDS & NPS 80CCD

Compute exact House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption under Rule 2A (least of actual HRA, rent paid minus 10% salary, or 50% salary in Metro cities / 40% Non-Metro). Estimate quarterly compounded Bank FD and RD maturity with Section 194A TDS thresholds (₹50,000 regular / ₹1,00,000 senior citizens), and plan retirement pensions with extra ₹50,000 NPS tax savings u/s 80CCD(1B).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How is ₹12.75 Lakh salary tax-free in the New Tax Regime for FY 2026–27? expand_more

For a salaried employee earning ₹12,75,000 gross annual salary, the ₹75,000 Standard Deduction reduces net taxable income to ₹12,00,000. Under the New Tax Regime slabs, the ₹60,000 full tax rebate under Section 87A/156 eliminates the entire slab tax on ₹12,00,000 (excluding special-rate capital gains), resulting in ₹0 tax payable.

How much deduction is needed for the Old Tax Regime to beat the New Regime? expand_more

Under Tax Year 2026–27 slabs, the New Tax Regime benefits most taxpayers with moderate deductions. For salaried incomes of ₹15 Lakh and above, combined eligible Chapter VI-A and HRA deductions (such as ₹1.5L under Section 80C, ₹50k under NPS 80CCD(1B), ₹25k–₹50k under 80D health insurance, and substantial HRA or Section 24(b) home loan interest) typically need to exceed ₹5.9 Lakh to ₹8+ Lakh depending on your income slab for the Old Regime to yield lower tax.

How do I reverse-calculate GST from an MRP / GST-Inclusive amount? expand_more

Select "GST Inclusive (Reverse Extract GST)" mode in the GST Calculator. The formula used is: Net Base Value = Gross Inclusive Amount × [100 / (100 + GST Rate)], and GST Tax = Gross Inclusive Amount − Net Base Value. For example, ₹1,180 inclusive of 18% GST has a ₹1,000 base price and ₹180 total GST (₹90 CGST + ₹90 SGST).
